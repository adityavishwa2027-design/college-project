## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, progressive vision of higher education operations. It merges contemporary technological clarity with the timeless prestige of university life. The interface balances high-density administrative utility with calm, breathable aesthetic restraint.

### Aesthetic Foundation
The style merges **Modern Editorial Minimalism** with **Luminous Soft-Glassmorphism**:
- **Tone & Persona:** Dignified, intellectual, serene, organized, and encouraging.
- **Visual Tenor:** Luminous off-white and pale cream canvases layered with translucent, tinted crystalline cards, crisp typographic contrast, and ultra-fine chromatic borders.
- **Emotional Intent:** Dispelling collegiate anxiety and administrative clutter by delivering an environment that feels orderly, inspiring, and distinctly collegiate.

## Colors

The color palette is built on soft, pigmented pastel washes over warm, mineral canvas neutrals, moving away from harsh pure whites and cold default grays.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#7B68EE` — Soft Iris Lavender):** Drives primary actions, active navigational states, and key student milestones. Conveys wisdom, focus, and modern intellect.
- **Secondary (`#68A5D8` — Powder Sky Blue):** Applied to informational notices, schedule blocks, timeline indicators, and secondary navigation anchors.
- **Tertiary (`#88B395` — Pale Sage Herb):** Signifies academic validations, active registrations, approvals, and holistic wellness updates.
- **Neutral (`#F9F8F6` — Alabaster Cream):** Canvas foundation providing depth and warmth beneath translucid surfaces.

### Semantic & Departmental Palette
Soft tinted tokens differentiate fields of study and student interests without visual noise:
- **Arts & Humanities / Cultural:** Warm Buttercream Peach (`#FCEADE`, text `#7A4B1A`)
- **Computer Science & AI:** Soft Iris Lavender Wash (`#EDE9FE`, text `#4F46E5`)
- **Mechanical Engineering / Robotics:** Powder Sky Blue Tint (`#E0F2FE`, text `#0369A1`)
- **Biotechnology / Sciences:** Pale Sage Mist (`#DCFCE7`, text `#166534`)
- **Business School & Careers:** Champagne Rose (`#FCE7F3`, text `#9D174D`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes modern utilitarian clarity with collegiate heritage.

### Font Pairing
- **Headlines (`Newsreader`):** Infuses campus editorial dignity, prestige, and literary warmth into portal greetings, major dashboard sections, and milestone awards.
- **Interface & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean optical geometric balance for timetables, GPA indexes, departmental filters, forms, and metadata tags.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- Display and `headline-lg` styles utilize italic variations sparingly on accent keywords (e.g., *Dean’s List*, *Upcoming Symposium*).
- All labels and badges maintain uppercase or title-case formatting with positive letter spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to optimize readability at compact sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs an asymmetric, fluid 12-column grid system designed to support dense academic data without overcrowding.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`, constrained to a maximum content width of 1440px. Left-side navigation occupies a fixed rail (260px) or 2 grid columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Sidebar transitions into a collapsible floating frosted drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1rem`. Multi-pane dashboards collapse into single vertical card streams.

### Layout Principles
- **Atmospheric Whitespace:** Generous vertical padding (`space-xl`) between section clusters gives complex student schedules room to breathe.
- **Card Clustered Grids:** Related data points (e.g., credits completed, current GPA, attendance) group into uniform micro-surfaces using `space-md` gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism, semi-translucent surface tiers, and tinted ambient glow borders rather than harsh drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F9F8F6` Alabaster base.
- **Tier 1 (Base Cards & Modules):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` mixed with `rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Modals & Floating Menus):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`. Casts a soft ambient glow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(104, 165, 216, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 3 (Active Focus & Drag States):** Lifted slightly with a multi-layered boundary: `0 0 0 2px rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.3)`, followed by a soft ambient shadow `0 16px 40px rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.12)`.

### Chromatic Glow Borders
Interactive cards feature subtle directional gradients on borders (`1px solid linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8), rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.2))`), giving the edges a translucent, mineral luster.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages balanced, organic curves (Level 2: 0.5rem base) to maintain a modern, friendly, yet rigorous institutional quality.

### Geometry Breakdown
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards, Modules, and Display Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px).
- **Hero Containers, Modals, Flyouts (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Pill Elements (Chips, Tags, Status Badges):** Full circular radius (`9999px`) to emphasize lightness and fluid categorization.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Soft Iris (`#7B68EE`) fading imperceptibly to `#6C5CE7`, white text, `height: 2.75rem`, padding `0 1.25rem`, radius `0.5rem`. Subtle lavender shadow `0 4px 14px rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.3)`. On hover: lifts 1px with increased glow.
- **Secondary:** Frosted neutral `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.2)`, text `#4A437F`. On hover: background becomes `rgba(237, 233, 254, 0.5)`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** No fill or border. Text in muted slate, transitioning to Iris with an underlining indicator on hover.

### Filter Pills & Badges
- **Shape:** Full pill shape (`9999px` radius), height `1.75rem`, typographic spec `label-sm`.
- **Department/Major Pill Styling:** Subtle pastel fills with deep coordinating text colors:
  - *Computer Science:* Background `#EDE9FE`, text `#4F46E5`.
  - *Mechanical Engineering:* Background `#E0F2FE`, text `#0369A1`.
  - *Arts & Humanities:* Background `#FCEADE`, text `#7A4B1A`.
  - *Biotechnology:* Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#166534`.
  - *Business School:* Background `#FCE7F3`, text `#9D174D`.
- **Interactive Interest Filters:** Outlined in `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` when inactive; fill becomes solid primary tint with an active dot indicator when toggled.

### Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** Frosted glass backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`), `1rem` radius, padded with `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- **Dividers:** Minimal, translucent lines (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`) to separate internal metadata sections without breaking card flow.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Height `2.75rem`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, border `1px solid rgba(104, 165, 216, 0.25)`, radius `0.5rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border color shifts to `#7B68EE` accompanied by a soft halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(123, 104, 238, 0.15)`.

### Tabbed Filtering
- Segmented pills enclosed in a frosted track (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`). Active tabs feature an Alabaster White pill with an ambient shadow, gliding along the track via smooth spring animations.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **GPA / Grade Milestone Dial:** Thin circular rings tinted with Pale Sage and Iris, framing Newsreader serif metrics.
- **Course Schedule Timeline Strip:** Hour blocks styled with translucent departmental pastels, stacked vertically with rounded left borders signaling room status.
